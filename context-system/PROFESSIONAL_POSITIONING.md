## 7. Architectural mindset and seniority

The website should communicate seniority through demonstrated technical judgment rather than through adjectives or inflated titles.

The current focus is not limited to dataset preparation, experimental modeling or model training. It includes selecting and evaluating appropriate technologies, working through architectural trade-offs, and implementing and evolving the software that brings ML and AI into real products.

When describing future AI systems, Victor's intended contribution includes determining viable modeling and integration strategies, setting engineering constraints, collaborating on technical direction, and ensuring that specialist implementation meets the system's requirements. This does not mean that Victor no longer codes or that formal people-management responsibilities necessarily exist.

Show the following decision dimensions in project narratives wherever concrete evidence exists:

- **Quality:** model behavior, task success, evaluation, failure modes and human review.
- **Latency and throughput:** response times, service boundaries and orchestration overhead.
- **Cost:** token consumption, inference costs and infrastructure trade-offs.
- **Reliability:** error diagnosis, observability, quality gates and production monitoring.
- **Security and privacy:** access and integration constraints, PII safeguards and data handling.
- **Scalability and maintainability:** modular design, separation of concerns, independent deployment and clear interfaces.

Do not imply every project has formal SLOs, quantified latency improvements, a specific scale of infrastructure, or particular architectural mechanisms unless documented. These dimensions are a framework for explaining decisions, not a license to invent results.
