Who I am

An ML Engineer who builds systems that ship.

I'm Victor Amarante — a Senior Machine Learning Engineer working on LLMs & agentic systems. I started in Statistics at UFPE's CCEN — probability, linear algebra, numerical calculus — and transferred to Information Systems at the CIn/UFPE to pair that math with real systems engineering.

The progression was a natural one: statistics → classical machine learning → deep learning → LLMs → agentic systems. Each layer still runs underneath the next. It's why I can reason about what actually happens inside a model and put it in production across healthcare, fintech, legal and logistics.

Today my focus is autonomous intelligent systems: LLM orchestration, agentic workflows, and RAG/GraphRAG retrieval — built to be observable, evaluated and reliable under real traffic.
